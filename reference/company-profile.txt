INTRODUCTION




A cafe with a story worth
sharing
Twitch Cafe grew from a shared love of good coffee,
cafe hopping and the simple pleasure of a daily brew.
Exploring cafes around the world introduced us to
different roasters, brewing styles and the little details
that make each place memorable. These experiences
inspired us to create a space of our own cosy, personal
and welcoming.

A place to enjoy thoughtfully prepared coffee, catch up
with familiar faces or take a quiet moment for yourself.
That idea became Twitch Cafe in Petaling Jaya, where
coffee, culture and connection come together.

ABOUT US

Inspired by coffee, shaped by
experience
Our love for coffee deepened during five years in Brisbane,
where cafe visits were part of daily life. Working as a
barista in cafes and at day markets also gave us hands-on
experience in preparing coffee and welcoming customers.

Travels around the world introduced us to different coffee
traditions, flavours and spaces, adding to the inspiration
behind Twitch. Today, Twitch Cafe brings these experiences
to Petaling Jaya, pairing carefully prepared coffee with
familiar local favourites in a cosy, welcoming setting.

It’s our way of sharing what we love: good food, good
coffee and time spent together.

PHYSICAL PDF PAGE 4

OUR STORY


 Why we chose the name Twitch
 A name born from resilience.

  Both founders experienced difficulties with their hands,
  beginning with nerve twitching. What followed was a
  challenging chapter of struggle, recovery, patience and
  perseverance.

  Today, those same hands can prepare and brew coffee every
  day. We chose to transform a difficult memory into a name that
  reminds us of progress, gratitude and possibility.

  Twitch is more than a name. It is a reminder that something
  meaningful can grow from a difficult beginning, and keep
  making room for new beginnings.

THE FIRST CUP



The everyday ritual

INFLUENCES



A little of Australia, here in PJ

Our years in Brisbane inspired some of the
comforts you’ll find at Twitch: rich chocolate drinks
made with Australian-sourced dark chocolate
powder, warming spiced chai latte and toasted
banana bread served with butter.

Alongside these, we celebrate Malaysian
favourites, nasi lemak wrapped in banana leaf,
fried mee hoon and fried kuey teow. Plus a slice of
butter cake to enjoy with your coffee.

It’s a menu shaped by places we’ve called home
and flavours we love to share.

A space shaped by family


Family has helped make Twitch feel like home. The
